How this estimate works

We estimate one token per four characters. The comparison uses the average length of active note bodies capped at 600 characters, multiplied by up to five notes. Inactive notes and sessions are excluded. Original attachments, prompts, metadata, and answers are not included.

This is a sample comparison against sending all active note bodies in this vault, not measured MCP savings or a session-specific retrieval. Actual retrieval depends on the question and may need full sources. It does not measure total tokens saved, money saved, or answer quality.
