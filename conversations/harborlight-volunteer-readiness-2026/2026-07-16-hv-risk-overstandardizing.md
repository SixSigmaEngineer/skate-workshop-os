# Risk - a playbook could overstandardize human situations

## Meeting notes

- #O: Scenario guidance can create false certainty.
- Community needs rarely match a perfect script, and volunteers must recognize when context, safety, or dignity requires escalation to a person with deeper experience.
- #P: Prescriptive scripts may suppress curiosity and miss important context.
- #Q: How will every scenario communicate uncertainty and escalation conditions?
- #A: Require a human-judgment checkpoint and named escalation path on every card.
- The playbook will guide conversations, not automate eligibility or final advice.
