# How might we make the safe next step obvious

## Meeting notes

- #Q: How might we show when guidance was reviewed, by whom, and with what confidence?
- How might we let every volunteer practice the same essential scenarios?
- How might we preserve expert judgment without turning it into inflexible policy?
- How might we make escalation feel like good service rather than failure?
- How might we make the safe next step obvious without removing human judgment?
- #A: Test one reviewed scenario card with a new volunteer and an experienced coach.
- Prototype one guided scenario and one escalation path.
