# Experienced volunteers keep trusted guidance in personal notes

## Meeting notes

- #O: Experienced volunteers maintain sticky notes and private documents with preferred contacts, warning signs, and wording that works.
- Their guidance is valuable but unavailable to the next shift and difficult to review systematically.
- The most useful guidance is contextual and currently trapped with individuals.
- #P: New volunteers receive facts without the judgment that makes those facts usable.
- #A: Capture five expert walkthroughs as scenario notes with rationale.
- #Q: How can contributors express uncertainty rather than presenting every note as a rule?
