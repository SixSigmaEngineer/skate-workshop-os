# Community quote - every handoff asks me to start over

## Meeting notes

- #O: Participant said: “Every handoff asks me to start over. By the third person, I wonder whether anyone heard me the first time.”
- This fictional composite quote captures the emotional cost that cycle-time metrics miss: repetition can feel like the system is not listening.
- Repetition is interpreted as a lack of listening and coordination.
- #P: A service process intended to help can unintentionally erode dignity and trust.
- #Q: What would make a person feel carried forward rather than passed along?
- #A: Use community-defined trust measures in the pilot evaluation.
