# Bridge insight - fragmented coordination, not staff commitment, drives delay

## Meeting notes

- #I: Harborlight does not have a motivation problem.
- #I: It has a continuity problem.
- Duplicate intake resets context, invisible referral status breaks feedback, and private resource lists fragment organizational knowledge.
- Staff compensate through effort, but effort cannot create a shared service journey.
- The strongest staff workarounds all compensate for missing continuity.
- #P: More training alone will not repair disconnected handoffs.
- #Q: What is the minimum shared memory needed to preserve continuity safely?
- #A: Adopt continuity, consent, and visible status as the pilot design principles.
- Use fragmented coordination as the working root-cause frame.
