# Partner eligibility rules change faster than staff playbooks

## Meeting notes

- #O: Program eligibility, required documents, service areas, and waitlist rules change frequently.
- Updates arrive through email and individual relationships, leaving staff and volunteers with different versions of what each partner currently accepts.
- Staff use personal notes and inbox searches to verify current eligibility rules.

- #A: Create an owner and review date for every partner-service note.
