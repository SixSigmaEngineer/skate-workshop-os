# Families enter the same information on four disconnected forms

## Meeting notes

- #O: Contact details, household size, language, income range, and service needs are captured separately by phone intake, the case-management spreadsheet, the referral email, and the partner's own form.
- Corrections rarely propagate, so staff reconcile conflicting versions by phone.
- The same household facts appear on four forms owned by different teams.
- #P: Duplicate entry creates delay, inconsistency, and unnecessary disclosure.
- #A: Count repeated fields and correction calls across ten recent referrals.
- #Q: What is the smallest consented dataset that can support a warm handoff?
- Intake Coordinator to inventory fields and identify the true minimum.
