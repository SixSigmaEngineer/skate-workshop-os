# Recommendation - pilot a consent-aware warm handoff

## Meeting notes

- #R: Run a two-week pilot with three fictionalized partner pathways.
- #R: Use a minimal intake summary, explicit sharing choices, and a four-state referral loop.
- Measure repeated questions, time to acknowledgement, unresolved referrals, and whether community reviewers feel the experience preserves dignity.
- #A: Prototype with synthetic households and three partner roles.
- Compare the warm handoff with today's email-and-spreadsheet baseline.
- #Q: Does continuity improve without collecting or exposing more information?
- Recruit three partner representatives and two community reviewers.
- Define baseline measures and the stop conditions.
