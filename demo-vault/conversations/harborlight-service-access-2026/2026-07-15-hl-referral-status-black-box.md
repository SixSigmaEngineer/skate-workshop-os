# Referral status disappears after the handoff

## Meeting notes

- #O: After Harborlight sends a referral, staff cannot tell whether the partner received it, contacted the family, placed them on a waitlist, or closed the request.
- Case managers chase updates by email while families wait without knowing what happens next.
- Referral emails leave Harborlight with no shared acknowledgement or status.
- #P: Invisible status creates waiting, duplicate follow-up, and preventable drop-off.
- #Q: Could a four-state loop—sent, received, contacted, resolved—restore visibility?
- #A: Measure unanswered referrals and status-chasing messages for two weeks.
- What status can partners share without exposing unnecessary client information?
