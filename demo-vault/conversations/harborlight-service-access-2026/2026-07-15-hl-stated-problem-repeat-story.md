# Stated problem - families repeat their story at every handoff

## Meeting notes

- #O: Families seeking housing, food, legal, and employment support repeatedly explain the same circumstances to Harborlight intake staff, a case manager, and each partner organization.
- The repetition consumes staff capacity and asks people to relive difficult experiences before they receive help.
- A family may repeat the same core story four times during one service journey.
- #P: Each handoff resets context instead of carrying it forward with consent.
- #Q: Which information is genuinely required at each step, and what can follow the family safely?
- #A: Map one real service journey from first call through confirmed partner connection.
- Program Director to select three anonymized journeys for process mapping.
- Where does consent need to be renewed rather than assumed?
