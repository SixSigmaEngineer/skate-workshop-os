# Experienced case managers maintain private resource lists

## Meeting notes

- #O: Harborlight's most experienced case managers keep personal spreadsheets of trusted contacts, workarounds, and current program details.
- The knowledge helps families, but it is invisible to new staff and disappears when someone is unavailable.
- High-value service knowledge exists, but ownership is individual rather than organizational.
- #P: New staff repeat research while proven pathways remain trapped in private files.
- #A: Compare three resource lists and capture a shared, reviewable core.
- #Q: How can contributors retain context and confidence levels, not just links?
