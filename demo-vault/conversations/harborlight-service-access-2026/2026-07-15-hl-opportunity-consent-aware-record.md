# Opportunity - a consent-aware community service record

## Meeting notes

- #S: A minimal, consent-aware record could carry verified household facts, stated needs, sharing permissions, and referral status across Harborlight's workflow.
- #S: It is not a central case file for every detail; it is a continuity layer that shares only what a person approves and a partner needs.
- The highest-value shared fields are few: contact preference, needs, consent, and status.
- #P: A larger record would increase privacy risk without improving the handoff.
- #Q: Can a minimal continuity layer work with existing partner tools?
- #A: Prototype with synthetic data before any live-client test.
