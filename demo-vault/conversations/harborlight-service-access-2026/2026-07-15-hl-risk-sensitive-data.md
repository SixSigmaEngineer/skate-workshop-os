# Risk - coordination could expose sensitive family information

## Meeting notes

- #O: Better coordination must not become broader surveillance.
- Housing status, legal concerns, immigration questions, health needs, and financial stress can be deeply sensitive.
- A shared workflow that collects too much or obscures consent would create harm even if it saves time.
- #P: Convenience can quietly expand collection and access beyond what service delivery requires.
- #Q: Can a person see, change, and revoke what Harborlight is permitted to share?
- #A: Define field-level purpose, retention, access, and consent before prototyping.
- Use synthetic data for the first pilot and require explicit human review before any external sharing.
- Privacy Lead to approve the minimum-data checklist and deletion rules.
