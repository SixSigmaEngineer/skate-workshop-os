# Recommendation - pilot a two-week volunteer readiness sprint

## Meeting notes

- #R: Test five scenario cards with one new-volunteer cohort for two weeks.
- #R: Compare confidence, answer accuracy, escalation quality, and coach time with the previous cohort.
- Update cards from observed questions rather than expanding the orientation deck.
- #A: Select five frequent, consequential scenarios and assign content owners.
- Practice each scenario before independent service.
- #Q: Does structured practice improve confidence without producing false certainty?
- Recruit six volunteers and two experienced coaches.
- Define baseline and stop conditions before the sprint.
