# Opportunity - a living scenario-based volunteer playbook

## Meeting notes

- #S: A reviewable playbook could organize guidance around common situations rather than departments.
- #S: Each scenario would show a safe next step, escalation criteria, source, owner, confidence, and last-reviewed date while linking back to the underlying evidence.
- Volunteers ask scenario questions, while current training is organized as policy categories.
- #P: A static manual hides freshness and uncertainty.
- #Q: Can the playbook guide judgment without pretending every situation is predictable?
- #A: Build five scenario cards from real but anonymized questions.
