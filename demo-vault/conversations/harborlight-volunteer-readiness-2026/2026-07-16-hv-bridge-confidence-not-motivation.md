# Bridge insight - the readiness gap is confidence, not motivation

## Meeting notes

- #I: Volunteers are motivated and cautious.
- #I: They hesitate because official content lacks freshness signals, shadowing is inconsistent, and expert judgment is not captured.
- The system asks them to choose between speed and safety without enough support.
- Every major workaround attempts to establish whether guidance can be trusted.
- #P: More orientation slides will not create situational confidence.
- #Q: What would make the safe next step obvious in common scenarios?
- #A: Use confidence, content freshness, and escalation clarity as design principles.
- Reframe readiness as a knowledge-confidence problem.
