# Stated problem - volunteers need six weeks to feel ready

## Meeting notes

- #O: New volunteers complete orientation in one day, yet most need about six weeks before they feel comfortable answering common service-navigation questions without finding an experienced staff member.
- Orientation completion and real-world readiness are treated as the same milestone.
- #P: Volunteers spend weeks uncertain about what they can answer safely.
- #Q: Which situations create the greatest hesitation after orientation?
- #A: Track questions and escalations from the next volunteer cohort.
- Volunteer Coordinator to baseline time-to-confidence for the next cohort.
