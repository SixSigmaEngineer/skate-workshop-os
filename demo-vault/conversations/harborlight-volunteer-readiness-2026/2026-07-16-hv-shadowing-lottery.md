# Shadowing quality depends on who is scheduled

## Meeting notes

- #O: Some new volunteers observe a wide variety of calls with a skilled coach; others spend their shift on routine tasks with little explanation.
- There is no shared scenario checklist, so the experience varies by schedule rather than learning need.
- Two volunteers in the same cohort can receive completely different practice.
- #P: Readiness depends on chance access to an experienced coach.
- #Q: What five scenarios should every volunteer practice before working independently?
- #A: Compare shadowing logs across the last three cohorts.
