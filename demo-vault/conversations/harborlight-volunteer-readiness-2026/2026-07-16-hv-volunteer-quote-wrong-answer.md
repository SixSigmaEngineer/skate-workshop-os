# Volunteer quote - I was afraid to give the wrong answer

## Meeting notes

- #O: Participant said: “I wanted to help, but I was afraid to give the wrong answer and make someone’s day harder.”
- This fictional composite quote shows that hesitation is often responsible caution, not disengagement.
- Volunteers pause because they understand the consequences of incorrect guidance.
- #P: Training measures recall but does not create confidence in judgment.
- #Q: What evidence tells a volunteer an answer is current and safe?
- #A: Include confidence and escalation comfort in the readiness measures.
