# Harborlight Service Access Workshop 2026

## Focus

Fictional one-day workshop for Harborlight Community Resource Network, a regional nonprofit connecting families with housing, food, legal, and employment services. Staff care deeply, but families repeat sensitive information at each handoff and nobody can see referral status across partner organizations.

## Readout Notes

- Primary pain: families repeat the same story across intake, case management, and partner referrals.
- Secondary pain: referral status becomes invisible after a handoff.
- Human signal: “Every handoff asks me to start over.”
- Reframe: the constraint is fragmented service coordination, not staff commitment.
- Opportunity: a consent-aware community record and warm-handoff status loop.
- Guardrail: minimize sensitive data and keep people in control of what is shared.
- Decision: test the workflow before selecting a new platform.
- First experiment: a two-week warm-handoff pilot with three partner organizations.
