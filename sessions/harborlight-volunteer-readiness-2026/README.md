# Harborlight Volunteer Readiness Workshop 2026

## Focus

Fictional workshop examining why new Harborlight volunteers take six weeks to feel confident answering community questions. Training is enthusiastic, but current service knowledge lives in slide decks, inboxes, and the memory of experienced volunteers.

## Readout Notes

- Primary pain: volunteers cannot tell which resource guidance is current.
- Secondary pain: shadowing quality depends on who happens to be scheduled.
- Human signal: “I wanted to help, but I was afraid to give the wrong answer.”
- Reframe: the bottleneck is knowledge confidence, not volunteer motivation.
- Opportunity: a scenario-based, reviewable service-navigation playbook.
- Guardrail: playbooks must preserve judgment and clear escalation paths.
- Decision: pilot a two-week readiness sprint with one volunteer cohort.
- First experiment: five common scenarios, named content owners, and confidence measures.
